Frequently Asked Questions

What is Circumvent AI?

Circumvent AI is a technology and creative studio in India. We build AI agents, Android apps, web portals and machine learning solutions, and we create social media marketing content and digital wedding invitations.

What is an AI agent and what can it do for my business?

An AI agent is a smart digital assistant that works on your own business data. It can prepare daily sales reports, analyse sales, profit and customer trends, handle repetitive work like invoice checks, stock alerts and data entry, and connect with your existing admin panel.

Can an AI agent check student assignments automatically?

Yes. We have built an Assignment Checking Agent that helps teachers check student work faster, so less time goes into manual checking.

Can AI analyse data directly from my database?

Yes. We have built a Database Analysis Agent that reads your backend data directly and turns it into simple reports and insights, without manual Excel work.

Can I control a physical product or machine from a mobile app?

Yes. We build custom mobile apps that control and monitor your device, machine or product from your phone, with on/off control, settings, schedules, live status and alerts.

What is machine learning in a physical product?

It means an ML model runs inside your device or product and learns from sensor data. We can also fine-tune the model with real sensor data so it works accurately in your product.

What is an office local server and why would I need one?

An office local server is a private server set up inside your office. It keeps your files, internal apps and business data under your own control, and your team can use it on your own network.

Do you build Android apps and web portals?

Yes. We build custom Android apps and secure, scalable web portals and dashboards for businesses, from design and backend to launch.

Which business marketing services do you offer?

We create business promotional posts and reels, product and service promotional reels, brand promotion content, and manage social media ads including campaign setup, targeting and promotion.

Which wedding services do you offer?

We create wedding video cards, digital wedding invitations, wedding invitation reels and save the date videos. They are delivered digitally and are ready to share on WhatsApp and social media.

How much does a project cost?

The cost depends on what you need, such as the type of service, features and timeline. Send us your idea through the contact form and we will share a clear quote.

Can I order only one service?

Yes. You can choose a single service or combine several, for example a wedding video card with an invitation reel, or an AI agent with a web portal.

How do I start a project?

Fill the contact form on this page with your idea. We will discuss the details and share a plan, and then start the work.
